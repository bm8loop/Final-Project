Getting to know our students

This app keeps a small directory of Rocky Mountain High School students and shows how they are connected: where they live compared with school, what they are interested in, and who might get along.

How it works

  • Everything is saved in three CSV files: students.csv for student records, interests.csv for the list of interests, and student_interests.csv for which student chose which interest.
  • When an address is saved, the free U.S. Census Bureau address lookup turns it into latitude and longitude. The distance from school (1300 W Swallow Rd) is the straight-line distance in miles, not the driving distance.
  • The friendship score is a simple formula for exploring the data, not a real prediction. The Friends page shows exactly how each part of the score is worked out.
